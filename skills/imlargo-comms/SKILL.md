# Comms

> Builds on `imlargo` (its Writing rules) and `imlargo-anti-slop`. Covers messages between people at work. Issues, PRs and commits follow `imlargo-workflow`; posts and articles aren't messages. A personal voice skill, when installed, wins on tone and wording.

## Principles

- **Point first.** The first line says why you're writing: the answer, the decision, the ask, or the news. Context comes after, and only what the reader needs to act.
- **One message, one purpose.** Two unrelated asks are two messages, or a numbered list announced up front ("Two things:").
- **Make the ask explicit.** Who does what, by when. "Can you review the PR by Thursday? It blocks the release." beats "Let me know your thoughts."
- **Write in the reader's frame.** What they know, what they care about, in their words. Non-technical readers get the effect and the decision, not the mechanism.
- **Specific over polite filler.** Numbers, names, dates, links. Thanks names what the person did. A sentence that would fit any message gets cut.
- **Short.** Most messages fit on a phone screen without scrolling. Longer content goes in a doc, linked.
- **Honest.** Only facts you can back. Never promise a date or a fix that isn't verified. Name what you don't know yet and when you will.
- **Own problems in one line.** A delay or a mistake: what happened, plainly, then the next step and when. No excuse paragraphs, no over-apologizing.
- **Warm, not effusive.** Courteous and direct, the way you'd say it to a colleague's face.
- **Match the thread.** Reply in the conversation's language and roughly its formality. Default to English.

## Before Drafting

1. Who reads it, what they already know, and what they should do or decide after reading.
2. The facts: read the thread, ticket, PR or doc it's about. Never fill a gap with an invented date, number, name or status: leave a `[to confirm: ...]` placeholder.
3. The channel: it sets length and format (see [channels](references/channels.md)).
4. Ask imlargo only when the purpose or a key fact is missing and can't be found. Don't ask about tone the thread already shows.

## Output

- Deliver the message ready to paste, nothing around it. Email adds a subject line.
- Placeholders for anything imlargo must fill or confirm, in brackets, listed in one line after the message.
- One version. Alternatives only when asked, or when the right tone truly depends on something you can't know (then two, labeled by when to use each).
- Run it through `imlargo-avoid-ai-writing` before delivering.

## Never

- Openers and closers that say nothing: "I hope this email finds you well", "Hope you're doing great!", "Just checking in", "Just wanted to follow up", "Please don't hesitate to reach out", "Looking forward to hearing from you", "Thanks in advance".
- Stiff or passive-aggressive formulas: "Kindly", "Per my last email", "As previously mentioned", "Dear Sir/Madam".
- Hedge stacks ("I was just wondering if maybe we could possibly..."), over-apologizing, and self-deprecation that undercuts the point.
- Effusive praise or gratitude ("You're amazing!!", "I can't thank you enough") to professional contacts.
- Corporate filler: "circle back", "synergy", "touch base", "move the needle", "leverage", "going forward".
- A bare "hi" or "quick question?" waiting for a reply before the real message.
- Emoji strings, decorative bold, headers in a chat message, or a wall of bullets for something two sentences can say.
