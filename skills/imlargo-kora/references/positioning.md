# Positioning

## The Thesis

Most business software fails before it's written: the client asks for what they think they need, the provider builds it as asked, nobody questioned the premise. Providers who build well and consultants who think well exist; almost nobody does both for small and mid-size companies. Kora closes that gap.

One line: *Pensamos, diseñamos y construimos el software que tu negocio necesita.*

## Who It's For

Defined by situation, not industry:

- Companies that outgrew Excel, email and WhatsApp and need a real system.
- Companies where "we've always done it this way" no longer works.
- Founders with a product idea worth validating before overspending.
- The constant: a client who doesn't know for sure what to build. They need criterion, not hands.

Size: small and mid-size companies in Colombia. The decision maker is almost never technical.

## The No-Client

- Knows exactly what they want and wants cheap hands to execute it.
- Buys on price.
- Needs a provider who says yes to everything.

Flag a lead that matches to imlargo instead of preparing a proposal.

## Differentiators

Shown, never claimed. As phrases they're noise; as proof (cases, testimonials, numbers) they're a moat.

- Every client came by referral.
- The client decides with whoever builds: no salesperson selling and junior executing.
- Kora says no when the request isn't the need.
- One or two projects at a time.
- Delivered systems keep running unsupervised.

Never use what every competitor says: "aliado estratégico", "calidad y confiabilidad", "metodologías ágiles", "transformación digital con IA".

## Voice and Look

- Clear, direct, confident without arrogance. Plain language, short sentences, provable claims. No buzzwords, hype or emojis.
- Business results, never stack or languages.
- Look: refined and sober. Contained palette of blacks, grays and white, lots of air, strong type hierarchy, subtle animation. Speaking quietly is the premium signal.

## Coherence Test

Before any copy, offer or decision:

1. Could any competitor say this? Then it's noise.
2. Can it be proven, or only claimed? If only claimed, it goes.
3. Does it matter to the client, or only to Kora? If only to Kora, cut it.
