# Engagement Models

Prices, rates and minimums are imlargo's. Never write a figure imlargo didn't give; mark it `[precio: imlargo]`.

## Kora Studio

| Layer | Model | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Diagnosis | Short, fixed price | The entry door. Proves the difference, qualifies the client, and ends with clarity whether or not they continue with Kora. |
| Project | Closed price per phase, quoted after the diagnosis | Predictable for the client without signing an open-ended scope. |
| Partner plan | Monthly | Keeps evolving what was built. Earned, not imposed. |

- Never an hourly rate (it anchors to commodity) and never one fixed price for a whole custom build (the scope grows and eats the margin).
- Terms that matter as much as the price: an advance to start, payment against each delivered phase, scope closed in writing, changes quoted separately.
- Each phase ends with something concrete in the client's hands. If a phase doesn't convince them, they stop there without having spent the next phase's budget.

## Independent Senior Engineer

- A professional services contract with a monthly fee for an agreed dedication (part-time or full-time), tied to concrete deliverables and evaluated monthly. The client decides month by month whether to continue.
- The first phase is analysis and design; the scope freezes at its end and the detailed estimate becomes the committed timeline.
- Dedication is the speed lever: more dedication shortens the timeline.
- The monthly agreement is also the maintenance and support contract, with response and resolution times written down.
- Not exclusive unless agreed otherwise.

## Shared by Both

- **Ownership:** the client owns the custom application and every asset (code, business logic, credentials, infrastructure config). General-purpose reusable components imlargo uses across projects stay imlargo's, with a perpetual, irrevocable, free license for the client.
- **Data:** 100% the client's, in a standard database, exportable any time, in accounts in the client's name.
- **Third parties:** hosting and licenses are contracted directly by the client, in their name, and aren't part of the fee. Estimate their monthly cost in the diagnosis.
- **Roles:** imlargo owns architecture, technical decisions, requirements documentation, building, testing, deploying, docs, manuals, training and the ownership handover. The client sets priorities, provides access to information and people, names one point of contact, and formally accepts each deliverable.
- **Changes:** minor ones are absorbed. Anything that widens the frozen scope is documented, estimated in time and cost, and agreed before it's built, now or in a later iteration.
- **Warranty:** a stabilization period with bug fixes at no cost after each delivery.
- **Delays:** late inputs from the client move the timeline proportionally, and that's stated up front.
