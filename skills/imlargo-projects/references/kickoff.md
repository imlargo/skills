# Kickoff

Think first, build later: building fast on a wrong premise is the most expensive way to fail.

## Interview

Ask imlargo, batched as multiple choice where possible:

- **Problem and current process:** what hurts, how it's done today (Excel, WhatsApp, paper), and what result the business expects.
- **Scope, phases and non-goals:** what phase 1 must include, what's explicitly out, the order of phases, deadlines.

Fill everything else (users, data, integrations) from the conversation and existing material, and mark what's assumed.

## Define Deliverables

1. **Diagnosis** for the client, in business language: the problem, current process and bottlenecks, the proposed solution, phases with their scope, and what's out.
2. **Repo docs** for agents: `context.md`, the specs and the roadmap (see [context-file](context-file.md), [requirements](requirements.md)).
3. **Backlog** on GitHub from the roadmap (see [backlog](backlog.md)).

Nothing is built until imlargo approves the Define deliverables.
